    Golden rules for 10-minute games

    1. Checks, captures, threats — scan those three before every move, even on the clock.
    2. Castle before move 8 — an uncastled king loses 10-minute games all by itself.
    3. Don't send the queen out too early — she becomes a target (see The Golden Widow).
    4. Every pawn move opens a door — f3/g4-style moves invite The Two-Move Trap family.
    5. Found a good move? Look one second longer for a named checkmate.
